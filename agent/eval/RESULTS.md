## Unsafe quotes: 2

An unsafe quote is a quote issued where the label says the right outcome is a clarifying question or a refusal. The target is 0. It is not 0 here, and the cases are named:

| Case | Language | Gold | Got | Text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| en-09 | en | clarify | quote | `We want the hall next Friday for a farewell party of 70 people.` |
| hg-04 | hinglish | clarify | quote | `Agle hafte Saturday ko party karni hai, 80 log honge.` |

Quotes issued at the wrong price (gold says quote, but the extracted fields priced differently): en-02.

Why these happen: in each unsafe case the recorded model invents or misreads a field (a date for "next Friday", a year the customer never gave) with confidence above the 7000 bps gate, and the kernel then prices what it is given. The confidence gate cannot catch a confidently wrong read; only a better extractor, or confirm-back of the date and guest count before quoting, can. Neither exists yet.

## How this is measured

- **Sets:** `agent/eval/sets/en.jsonl`, `hinglish.jsonl` (37 cases total; `hinglish.jsonl` also holds the Devanagari cases, `lang: "hi"`). Each case has the text, gold fields, and a gold pipeline outcome (quote, clarify, or refuse + reason) against the named fictional card `fictional-banquet` (`rate-cards/fictional-banquet.json`). A test checks every gold outcome equals what the kernel returns for the gold fields.
- **Labelling convention:** a date with no year is gold-labelled with the next occurrence after the scenario clock (2026-11-07T10:00:00Z); a relative date ("next Friday") or an ambiguous numeric one ("10-11-12") is labelled `null` and the right outcome is a clarifying question, because the model is not told today's date.
- **Extraction metrics:** exact match per field over `service, date, guestCount, locality, language` (5 per case; a schema-invalid or failed extraction scores 0 of 5). Schema-valid rate is the share passing the strict validator. Mean confidence is over schema-valid extractions only. All percentages are integer basis points, rounded.
- **Pipeline metrics:** each case runs through the real `handleMessage` (extract, strict schema, confidence gate at 7000 bps, kernel, ledger) on a throwaway database. Outcome accuracy needs the same kind and, for refusals, the same reason. A missing-field refusal counts as "clarify".
- **Recordings:** `agent/eval/recordings/`, seeded by `pnpm --filter agent exec tsx src/eval/seed.ts` from `src/eval/recorded.ts`. Every case defaults to "the model returned the gold fields at 9000 bps"; the exceptions in that file are the injected mistakes.
